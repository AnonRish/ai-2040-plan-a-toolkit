# RVP-1 evidence bundle

A portable bundle contains a machine-readable passport, claim records, evidence
graph root, signed receipts or attestation references, model/workload
fingerprints, policy digest, environment manifest, and raw evidence where
disclosure permits.

A receiving verifier must validate integrity, then claim-level evidence
requirements, evidence maturity/independence, and expiry/revocation. A passport
does not upgrade an evidence level.
